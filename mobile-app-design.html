FV Bank

Fiat and crypto banking in one app

Live on Google Play

FV Bank redefines online banking by merging traditional and crypto transactions into a single seamless platform. For the mobile UI/UX, I focused on crafting a dual-mode experience—light and dark themes designed for different moods and environments. The design emphasizes clarity, security, and modernity, featuring intuitive dashboards, quick-transfer gestures, and real-time transaction updates. Every interaction was designed to feel fluid and professional, making digital banking both elegant and effortless across fiat and crypto worlds.

Mobility · Ride-sharing

Ridewise

Booking a ride in two taps

Ridewise is a next-generation rental and ride-sharing platform inspired by Blusmart’s eco-friendly model. My UI/UX approach centered on simplicity and speed—making booking rides a two-tap experience. The interface uses clean navigation, live tracking visuals, and dynamic fare cards for a smooth flow. With bold typography, bright call-to-actions, and responsive map animations, I designed the experience to feel smart, sustainable, and city-ready, empowering users to travel efficiently with confidence.

Web3 · Crypto wallet

Yug Wallet

Self-custody crypto with built-in KYC

Yug Wallet combines the power of decentralization with the safety of KYC verification, offering users the best of both worlds. In the UI/UX design, I focused on trust, transparency, and futuristic appeal—using geometric layouts, blockchain-inspired iconography, and soft animations that guide users through wallet creation, KYC, and transactions. The flow ensures crypto management feels personal yet protected, making Yug Wallet a modern evolution of secure digital ownership.

Astrology · Consultations

Wishtok

Horoscopes, consultations and reports in one app

Wishtok brings astrology closer to users with personalized horoscopes, consultations, and cosmic insights. For the UI/UX, I created a calming, mystical design language using celestial color gradients, minimal zodiac icons, and a card-based layout for easy navigation between predictions, astrologer chats, and reports. The interface feels intuitive yet magical, letting users explore their spiritual side through a clean, engaging, and emotionally resonant design.

Nayi Location

Property search that feels guided, not stressful

Nayi Location helps users discover properties to buy, rent, or lease with rich details and smart recommendations. I designed the UI/UX around clarity, credibility, and exploration—integrating map-based browsing, location tagging, and smart filters for price, amenities, and proximity. The visual tone uses professional, trustworthy aesthetics with a touch of warmth to make property search less stressful and more guided. Every screen is structured to help users find their next home faster and smarter.
